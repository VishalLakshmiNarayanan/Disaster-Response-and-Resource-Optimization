THE PROBLEM

The status quo
leaves people behind.

Traditional disaster response allocates resources proportionally to population — completely ignoring that some communities are far more vulnerable than others. Low-income, minority, and rural counties end up with the same per-capita allocation as wealthy suburban ones, even though their capacity to self-recover is drastically different.

THE SOLUTION

Three steps to equitable response.

01

Predict Demand

A two-stage XGBoost hurdle model first classifies which counties will have any demand (AUC 0.65), then estimates the magnitude using wind speed, distance, SVI, and demographic features.

XGBoost Hurdle Model SHAP
02

Optimize Allocation

A linear program minimizes weighted unmet demand subject to a supply cap and an equity constraint reserving 40% of resources for the most vulnerable counties (SVI ≥ 0.75).

Linear Program PuLP / CBC Equity Constraint
03

Protect the Vulnerable

Results are statistically validated with 10,000 bootstrap resamples. All equity improvements are significant at the 95% level. The system is robust to ±20% demand forecast uncertainty.
